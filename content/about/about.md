I'm a product designer who moves fluidly between marketplace products and fintech risk tooling — two worlds that both reward the same thing: clarity under constraint.

My background centers on product design for real estate and auto marketplaces, fintech card and fraud controls, and design systems — with a Figma-first workflow refined across Adobe XD, Photoshop, Illustrator, and InVision.

My work spans discovery, systems-level thinking, and pixel-level craft. I care about interfaces that hold up under real use — dense data, high-stakes decisions, and the everyday moments in between.

Outside of client and product work, I spend time refining design systems and exploring how motion and interaction can make complex tools feel effortless.

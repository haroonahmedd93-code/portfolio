# Font licences

All three faces are self-hosted and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- **League Gothic** (variable width, latin) — The League of Moveable Type / Google Fonts. Display face (rendered with a thin text-stroke to approximate a Black weight).
- **Instrument Sans** (variable, latin) — Rodrigo Fuenzalida, Jordan Egstad / Google Fonts. Text and UI face.
- **Mrs Saint Delafield** — Sudtipos / Google Fonts. Script fallback until the handwritten signature is vectorised.

Subset files come from the `@fontsource` packages (see package.json).
